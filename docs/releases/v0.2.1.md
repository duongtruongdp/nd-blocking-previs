# ND Blocking & Previs v0.2.1

## Freeform Timeline & NLE Editing

Version 0.2.1 focuses on reliable Master Timeline editing for previs and shot
planning.

### Highlights

- Freeform Master Timeline placement with intentional gaps.
- Independent Shot positioning without ripple movement.
- Snap On/Off for placement and editing precision.
- Non-destructive Shot trimming with source-bound and neighboring-Shot
  clamping.
- Multi-selection and group movement.

### Improvements

- More reliable Shot body dragging and edge-trim hit testing.
- Larger, screen-pixel-based trim interaction areas with NLE-style cursors.
- Trim gestures remain active while the pointer travels beyond the visual Shot
  bounds.
- Repeated extension and back-and-forth trim movement is calculated from the
  original gesture, avoiding cumulative frame drift.
- Graphics remain independently placeable in the Master Timeline.
- Improved low-zoom geometry and Marquee Selection stability.
- Existing undo/redo, duplicate, delete, and project persistence workflows
  remain available.

### Fixes

- Restored independent Shot movement after the first trim interaction pass.
- Prevented ordinary Shot-body clicks and drags from being misclassified as
  right-edge trims.
- Restored functional left-edge trimming while preserving right-edge timing.
- Fixed trim previews that became invalid when the pointer moved beyond the
  available source range.
- Preserved non-overlap behavior without moving neighboring Shots.

### Known Limitations

- The macOS Apple Silicon application is distributed outside the Mac App
  Store and is unsigned and not Apple-notarized.
- Windows installers are not published.
- Live Review and collaborative editing remain Beta workflow foundations and
  require environment-specific validation.
- Export performance and browser graphics behavior depend on the host system
  and available GPU resources.
